Refueler Share lets you transfer files to anyone without creating an account, entering
an email address, or trusting a third party with your documents. Files are encrypted
in your browser before they leave your device — the server receives sealed ciphertext
and nothing else. Access is gated by Bitcoin-native cryptographic tokens: the server
cannot open what it stores, cannot see who sent it, and cannot see who collected it.
When the transfer expires, it is struck off permanently. This is what private file
transfer looks like when it is built on Bitcoin from the ground up.
